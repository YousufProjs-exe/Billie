# Billie
Desktop Pet ! - Billie the cat :) 

Billie is a small desktop cat built with Godot. She lives on your desktop, walks around the screen, takes breaks, and reacts(meow) when you interact with her. The idea is to make her feel like a little companion instead of just another app running in the background.

[Try Billie](https://itch.io/18ANANA/billie) ~for windows

- **Pick Up** - Billie can be picked up ! 
- **Desktop roaming** — Billie walks around the screen and changes direction at the edges.
- **Idle animation** — she occasionally stops and plays her `turn_around` animation.
- **Click interaction** — single-click to stop her or get her walking again.
- **Meow** — single-click Billie to make her meow.
- **Spins** - double-click Billie and she spins ! 
- **Sleep** - triple click and she sleep (wakes up when disturbed).
- **Pixel-art character** — animated using Godot's `AnimatedSprite2D`.

## Built with

- GDScript

## What's next?

Billie is still a work in progress. Sleeping, waking up, and more little cat behaviors are planned, along with improvements to how she interacts with the desktop.
The goal is simple: make Billie feel a little more alive every time you see her on screen.
